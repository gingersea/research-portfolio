# Post-run wording clarification

The pre-run protocol and generated raw English report use the shorthand
"no external force" while specifying a quadratic vertical trajectory. Read
literally, that is wrong: a ballistic trajectory has constant gravitational
acceleration. The intended and mathematically implemented world has constant
gravity and no **additional** force, impulse or contact.

This clarification was written after the run. The original protocol, executed
source and generated raw report are retained byte-for-byte, with their original
hashes. No motion equation, image, intervention, label definition, metric or
exclusion changed. The numerical contract was always the explicitly prescribed
x(t), y(t), fixed radius and camera. Labels test deviations from that contract;
they are not independently established universal physical-validity labels.

For a clean citation use docs/CLOSEOUT_AND_PIVOT_2026-11-14.zh.md together with
the raw results and this note. This wording correction does not establish that
the synthetic examples represent the distribution of real generated failures.
