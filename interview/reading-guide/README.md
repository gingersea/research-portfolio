## Evidence boundaries / 证据边界

- This is an AI-assisted, source-checked interview study guide, not a systematic review or an experimental reproduction. Ginny Wong is a coauthor of the selected papers; no claim is made about her individual contribution or actual interview questions.
- Paper versions and publication status are recorded in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md). MemLens and AgentIdeaBench are treated as preprints. Findings are bounded to each paper's evaluated setting.
- Examples and proposed experiments are instructional material. They are not reported measurements, implemented extensions, or achievements of the portfolio owner.
- 原有项目介绍及证据边界见 [面试材料说明](../00_面试使用说明与证据边界.md)。优化项目不据此获得新的性能结论；视频物理一致性项目仍须按未完成项目介绍。
