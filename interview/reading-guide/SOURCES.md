## Selection and verification / 筛选与核验

The scope is the seven recommended core/recent readings plus two supporting papers from the preceding interview discussion. Selection emphasizes reasoning, scientific exploration, adaptation, multimodal evidence, memory, knowledge graphs, and causality. These nine distinct works were retained; no claim of comprehensive coverage is made.

The earlier discovery used the supplied interviewer identity, the ACL author index, institutional information, and exact paper titles. This update reuses that evidence, checks publication metadata against primary pages, and adds bilingual instructional explanations. Google Scholar and LinkedIn were not available for complete direct inspection; no citation totals, h-index, author-order contribution claims, or exhaustive publication counts are inferred from them.

## Interpreting the evidence / 如何使用这些证据

- R1–R7: selected methods/results/limitations were consulted; “read” does not mean every appendix was audited or every experiment reproduced.
- R8–R9: shorter supporting explanations use the verified high-level method descriptions. The exercises and mathematical examples are teaching material rather than reconstructions of the original experiments.
- Both language versions carry the same scientific qualifications. Translation does not create independent corroboration.
- Quantitative teaching examples are explicitly constructed. No new empirical measurements, model rankings, or reproduction results are claimed.
- Study recommendations and portfolio connections are the guide's analysis. They are not statements by the interviewer or confirmed interview questions.
